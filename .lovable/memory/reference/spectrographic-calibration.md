## Paper: Beber BC, Cielo CA (2012)
"Características da espectrografia de banda larga e estreita da emissão vocal de homens com laringe sem afecções"
Rev. CEFAC. 2012 Mar-Abr; 14(2):290-297

### Key findings applied to Orion v19:
- **F1/F2 intensity**: STRONG (48-53%) → keep tight bandwidths
- **F3 definition**: POOR (61%, p=0.002) → BW3 × 2.2 (~330Hz)
- **3.2kHz noise**: MUCH (73%, p=0.04) → aspiration noise layer (18%)
- **Whole-spectrum noise**: MUCH (70%) → voiced+aspiration source
- **Low-freq noise**: MEDIAN (68%) → moderate
- **Tracing regularity**: POOR (61-69%, p<0.001) → jitter×1.35, shimmer×1.2
- **Anti-resonance/damping**: MEDIAN (73-84%, p<0.001) → global LP damping at 4kHz
- **Whole-spectrum intensity**: WEAK (46%) → spectral tilt ×1.3
