# Palette's Journal - Critical UX/Accessibility Learnings

## 2026-07-03 - Localization Standard for Accessibility
**Learning:** The project uses Portuguese (PT-BR) as the primary language for user interface elements. Accessibility labels (aria-labels) and tooltips (titles) must follow this standard to maintain consistency and ensure a seamless experience for users of assistive technologies.
**Action:** Always provide `aria-label` and `title` in Portuguese (PT-BR) when modifying or adding components.
