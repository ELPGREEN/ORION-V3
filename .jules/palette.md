# Palette's UX Journal 🎨

## 2024-05-22 - Accessible Shopping Cart Interactions
**Learning:** Shopping cart controls (quantity adjustments and item removal) are critical interaction points that frequently lack semantic labels and focus visibility in rapid builds. Using standard `aria-label` and `focus-visible` rings ensures these high-intent actions are usable by all users.
**Action:** Standardize use of `aria-label` and `focus-visible:ring` for all icon-only buttons in transactional overlays like drawers and modals.
